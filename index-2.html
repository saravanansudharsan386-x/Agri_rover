Multifunctional Agricultural Robotics

Smarter farming.
Less manual work.

AGRI-BOT is a prototype concept designed to bring multiple routine farming tasks into one mobile platform: seed sowing, water spraying and weed-removal assistance, with microcontroller-based control and a path toward safer autonomous operation.

01 / Overview

One platform, multiple field tasks

The project explores how a compact, modular robot can assist small-scale farming. The final performance depends on the mechanical design, field conditions, calibration and testing.

🌱

Seed sowing

A seed dispenser is intended to release seeds at planned intervals. Seed spacing and depth require calibration for the chosen crop.

💧

Water spraying

A tank, pump and spray nozzle provide controlled water delivery to a selected area, helping avoid unnecessary spraying.

🌿

Weed removal

A mechanical weeding attachment is designed to assist removal of weeds. It needs crop-safe positioning and field testing.

02 / Features

Designed for practical farm assistance

📶

Wireless control

ESP32-based Wi-Fi control can support manual commands from a phone or browser interface.

🛞

Mobile chassis

A four-wheel chassis carries the payload and tools. Traction, ground clearance and stability should be checked on the target soil.

🧭

Obstacle awareness

A distance sensor can provide a safety input to slow or stop the robot when an obstacle is detected.

🔧

Modular attachments

Seed, spray and weeding units can be mounted as separate modules for maintenance and testing.

🔋

Power monitoring

Battery voltage monitoring can help the operator understand power status during field operation.

🧪

Prototype-first approach

Each function can be tested independently before combining functions into a full field workflow.

Literature review summary

Existing agricultural robotics research explores task automation such as seed placement, irrigation, weeding and wireless operation. Inspired by these areas, AGRI-BOT brings multiple farm-assistance mechanisms into one prototype and uses an ESP32-based control system. The project focuses on modular construction and practical testing; measurable benefits such as time saved, water use and task accuracy should be established through controlled experiments.

05 / Future scope

From prototype to smarter field operation

📷

AI crop monitoring

Add a camera and computer-vision model to monitor crop condition or identify visible plant stress, after collecting suitable data.

🛰️

Autonomous navigation

Explore GPS, row-following sensors or mapping for planned movement, with emergency stop and obstacle checks.

☀️

Solar-assisted charging

Evaluate a solar charging system based on the robot's measured power demand, battery chemistry and safe charge controller requirements.

📊

Field data dashboard

Record task status, battery level, distance travelled and water usage for later analysis.

🎯

Precision application

Use crop-row detection and calibrated dispensing to improve seed placement and targeted watering.

🧱

Rugged modular body

Improve weather protection, tool mounting, service access and stability for the intended terrain.

06 / Evaluation plan

What should be tested?

Seed placement

Measure seed spacing consistency and successful dispensing across repeated trials.

Water delivery

Measure flow rate and delivered volume over a defined time or area.

Obstacle response

Test detection distance, stopping response and repeatability under safe conditions.
